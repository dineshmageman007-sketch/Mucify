## Built on the work of others
Mucify is a friendly front end for some excellent open-source tools. Full licence information is in
[THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md).

- **sldl 2.6.0** (now Sockseek, formerly slsk-batchdl) by fiso64: the Soulseek batch downloader at the heart of the downloader step.
  <https://github.com/fiso64/sockseek>
- **rsgain** by complexlogic, based on loudgain by Matthias C. Hormann and Alessandro Ghedini: ReplayGain 2.0 tagging.
  <https://github.com/complexlogic/rsgain>
- **Mutagen** (quodlibet): reading and writing audio tags. <https://github.com/quodlibet/mutagen>
- **Flask / Werkzeug / Jinja** (Pallets), **Requests**, **pywebview** (Roman Sirokov), **PyInstaller**: the app framework and packaging.
- **Inno Setup** (Jordan Russell and Martijn Laan): the installer.
- **Microsoft Edge WebView2**: the window that shows Mucify's interface.

Thank you to the Soulseek community, to Qobuz for publishing track quality information, and to
chosic.com for the playlist exporter that many people use to get their starting CSV.
